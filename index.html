Shorts Publisher

Shorts Publisher is a small, private command-line tool that an independent developer uses to publish short educational videos to the developer's own YouTube channels and to review those channels' performance.

Not a public service. The tool is not offered to other users. Only the channel owner (the developer) signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 and authorizes access to their own channels.

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos (vertical, under 60 seconds) with their title, description, tags, category and scheduled publish time (videos.insert).
  • Reads and updates the metadata of the owner's own videos and channel (videos.list, videos.update, channels.list, channels.update, playlistItems.list).
  • Reads YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own videos (views, average view percentage, likes, comments, shares) to decide how to improve the next video.

What it does not do

  • No access to other people's channels or data.
  • No comments, likes, subscriptions or any automated engagement.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.

How it works

  1. The owner prepares the video files and metadata.
  2. The tool signs in once with Google OAuth 2.0 (installed-app flow with PKCE). The refresh token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  3. The tool calls the YouTube Data API to upload the video as scheduled, then reads YouTube Analytics 48 hours later.
